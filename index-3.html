ABOUT THE PROJECT

THE KOPC COMPANY

KOPC is a fictional technology brand created for this concept project. The goal: imagine gaming hardware with bold design and honest specs.

K↗
KOPC LABS

Built for play.

Our concept identity is sharp, simple, and performance-focused—without pretending untested hardware is already a champion.

Our principles

  • Performance first: sustained FPS matters more than a big spec number.
  • Cooling matters: heat control is part of performance.
  • Honest rankings: concept specs are never presented as measured results.
  • Player-focused: storage, battery health, touch response, and game compatibility count.
